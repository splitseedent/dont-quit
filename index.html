Your privacy

What we collect

Only what you type when you ask for help: your name if you give it, your phone number, and your message. Browsing the prayer and the daily word collects nothing about you.

Who sees it

Pastor and his care team at Touch My Soul Ministry — no one else. The inbox can only be opened with the ministry's own login. Your school receives counts only — how many requests of each kind — never your name, your number, or your words.

What we never do

We never sell your information or share it with advertisers. The alert that buzzes Pastor's phone carries no name, no number, and none of your words — only the kind of request. What you share does not affect your classes, your grades, or your standing at school.

How long we keep it

Until your request is answered and followed up. After that it is cleared from the active inbox. To have a request removed sooner, text Pastor at 317-956-4120 and ask.

How we count visits

We count anonymous visits so we can report how many people the app is reaching — counts of visits and taps only. We never store who visited, where you are, or what you read or wrote.
